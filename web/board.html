How points work

  1. 10 points for a breach, 5 for holding a breach off.
  2. A fight only counts when both devices report it: the attacker says “I got in”, the target’s own device says “I was breached”. A modified device cannot score alone.
  3. At most 3 fights between the same two players count each week, so nobody climbs by farming one friend.
  4. Level is as the device reports it. Rank is by points.

Get on the board

  1. On your T-Deck press G (or Options → World board).
  2. Press W, pick your Wi-Fi network and type its password.
  3. That’s it. It syncs every 15 minutes and soon after every fight. Nothing but your codename, faction, level and fights is sent.

No T-Deck yet? Install Cypher32 from your browser.
